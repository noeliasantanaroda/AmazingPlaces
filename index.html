Amazing Places

Today in Amazing Places we will talk about 5 beautiful places you should visit on Italy.

Introduction: Italy, officially known as the Italian Republic, is a country located in the heart of the Mediterranean, in southern Europe. It is bordered to the north by France, Switzerland, Austria and Slovenia, and is surrounded by the Adriatic, Ionian and Tyrrhenian Seas. Territory includes the islands of Sicily and Sardinia, the largest in the Mediterranean. Italy is renowned for its rich history, its contribution to the arts and sciences, and its influence on culture and gastronomy worldwide.More info.

1-Rome: the eternal city

Among the most touristic places in Italy, the first one that comes to mind is Rome. Do you know a city that has so many things to see that you need at least ten days to visit it properly? We don't, so get ready, you are going to be amazed with all that the eternal city has to offer. And above all, wear comfortable shoes, since the best way to get to know it is to walk, walk and walk!

Among the things to visit in Rome, you surely know the Colosseum, the Vatican or the Trevi Fountain, but what you don't know is that walking through the streets of the city there is something beautiful to admire in literally every corner.

We (and many more people!) are in love with Rome, and we believe that if you are planning to travel to Italy, the capital should be the first destination you should consider.

2-Pisa

Pisa conquers a place among the most touristic places in Italy thanks to its grandiose Piazza dei Miracoli. Every traveler dreams of taking a picture holding the Leaning Tower of Pisa, the main attraction of this square and of the whole city.

No one can resist the curiosity to see with their own eyes how it is possible that this tower does not fall to the ground with such pendency. For this reason, it has become one of the most iconic monuments in Italy, which many tourists include in their itinerary when visiting the country.

3-Pompeii

Among the most touristic places in Italy could not miss the incredible Pompeii. Surely you have heard about the history of this town, which in the first century A.D. was completely covered with lava from the Vesuvius volcano.

The city was preserved under the lava until today, and thanks to this, today we can see how an ancient Roman city looked like two thousand years ago. Interesting, isn't it? A tip we can give you is to book a guided tour in English, because being such a big site and with so much history, it is important to be accompanied by someone who can explain everything in the best way.

4-Venice:the charms of the Lagoon

Because of its beauty, Venice is one of the most visited places in Italy and in all of Europe. Being a UNIQUE PLACE IN THE WORLD, this city is a must on any trip to the belpaese.

Walking along the streets and bridges of Venice admiring the canals will seem like a dream, and if you get on a gondola, it will be even more so! Apart from its peculiarity, Venice is one of the most touristic places in Italy because it has dozens of beautiful attractions to visit. The most famous are the wonderful St. Mark's Basilica, the Doge's Palace, the Rialto Bridge and the La Fenice Theater.

If you want to know these iconic attractions not only of the city, but of the whole Italy, you already know that you have to organize a trip to Venice as soon as possible 😍.

5-Lake Como

Lake Como is one of the most touristic places in Italy, and we would add that it is also one of the most beautiful. The landscape of this lake is postcard-worthy, where charming villages, the beautiful color of the water, and the green mountains in the background give unforgettable views.

We believe that on a trip to Italy, especially if you move around Milan or in the north of the country, a visit to Lake Como is a MUST. In addition, we think it is an excellent excursion if you have a few days to visit, since the villages of Lake Como are small and you can see more than one in one day. The ones that in our opinion cannot be missed are Bellagio, Lecco, Varenna, Nesso and the city of Como.
